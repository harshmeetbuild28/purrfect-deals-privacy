Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

EpsilonPi Limited ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Purrfect Deals mobile application. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

We collect the following personal information when you create an account:

  • Display Name — used to personalise your experience
  • Email Address — used for account authentication and password resets
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication

We do not collect your location, payment details, contacts, or any sensitive personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account

  • To respond to support requests

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage — Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — app build and push notification delivery
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate links to Amazon products
  • AliExpress Affiliate — affiliate links to AliExpress products

When you tap "Buy Now", you are redirected to the retailer's website or app. Any purchase you make is subject to that retailer's own privacy policy and terms.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore, located in the United States. We use Firebase Authentication with industry-standard encryption. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. To request complete deletion of your data, email us at ronnieharsh13@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Purrfect Deals is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
